Privacy Policy – Abanoub Apps Watch Faces

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy covers the following Wear OS watch faces published on Google Play by Abanoub Apps: Orbit, Pulse, Stack and Aurum.

Data we collect

None. The watch faces do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or device data. They contain no code, no analytics, no advertising and no network access.

Data shown on the watch

The watch faces display information your watch already provides, such as the time, date, battery level, step count and, in Pulse, heart rate. This information is read from the watch and shown on its screen only. It never leaves your watch and is not saved by the watch face.

Complications

You can choose complications (small data widgets) from other apps to show on a watch face. Those apps provide their own data and are covered by their own privacy policies.

Children

The watch faces are not directed at children under 13 and collect no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
